# Problem Statement

## 1. Title

**Creator Marketplace for Hiring Video Editors, Designers, and Scriptwriters**

---

## 2. Domain

Digital Media, Content Creation, Freelance Marketplace

---

## 3. Who is the User? (2–3 User Types with Roles)

### Creator

Content creators, YouTubers, influencers, agencies, and businesses who need skilled professionals to complete creative projects.

### Freelancer

Video editors, graphic designers, scriptwriters, motion graphic designers, and thumbnail designers who offer their services and apply for projects.

### Admin

Manages the platform by monitoring users, approving or removing inappropriate content, handling reports, and maintaining system security.

---

## 4. What Problem Are We Solving?

The rapid growth of digital content creation has increased the demand for skilled professionals such as video editors, designers, and scriptwriters. However, many creators spend significant time searching for reliable freelancers through multiple social media platforms and messaging applications, making the hiring process slow and unorganized.

Freelancers also struggle to showcase their portfolios and connect with genuine clients. Existing freelance platforms often charge high service fees and are not specifically designed for content creators.

This project aims to provide a dedicated marketplace where creators can easily discover talented freelancers, review their portfolios, communicate directly, and hire the right person for their projects.

---

## 5. Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is a web-based Creator Marketplace that connects content creators with freelance professionals.

The application will provide:

* Secure user registration and login.
* Separate dashboards for Creators and Freelancers.
* Professional freelancer profiles with portfolios.
* Job posting and management for creators.
* Job application system for freelancers.
* Search and filtering based on skills, experience, and ratings.
* Ratings and reviews after project completion.
* Notifications for applications, messages, and hiring updates.
* Administrative panel for managing users, jobs, and platform activities.

---

---

## 7. User Roles & Permissions

### Admin

* Manage users
* Manage job postings
* Remove inappropriate content
* Monitor reports
* View platform statistics

### Creator

* Register and log in
* Create and update profile
* Post job requirements
* Search freelancers
* View portfolios
* Hire freelancers
* Chat with freelancers
* Provide ratings and reviews

### Freelancer

* Register and log in
* Create professional profile
* Upload portfolio
* Browse available jobs
* Apply for jobs
* Chat with creators
* Receive ratings and reviews

---

## 8. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

* Users can register and log in securely.
* Creators can post jobs successfully.
* Freelancers can create profiles and upload portfolios.
* Creators can search and filter freelancers.
* Freelancers can apply for jobs.
* Messaging between creators and freelancers works correctly.
* Hiring decisions can be completed through the platform.
* Administrators can effectively manage users and job postings.

---

## 9. Out of Scope

The first version of the project will not include:

* Online payment gateway integration
* Video conferencing
* Mobile application
* Multi-language support
* AI-based recommendations
* Live streaming
* Cryptocurrency payments

These features may be added as future enhancements.
